# Privacy and Data Processing

Clarion is intended to process customer-configured organization, brand, and report data. Before commercialization, operators should finalize:

- privacy policy and data retention terms
- subprocessors and AI-provider disclosures
- log retention and deletion policy
- data export and account deletion workflow
- regional hosting and regulatory commitments

This file exists so those product and legal controls are tracked in-repo as part of launch readiness.
